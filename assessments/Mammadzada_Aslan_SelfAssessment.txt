I wrote main.py end to end: validate(), prompt_for_cve() with its three-try
limit, gather(), open_references() and main(). In analyse.py I built the

What I learned: NVD sometimes lists the same reference URL twice, so
score_references() now keeps a list of URLs it has already seen and skips
repeats, otherwise the tool opened the same advisory in two browser tabs.
